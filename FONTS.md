Put these five files in app/src/main/res/font/ (lowercase and underscores only, per Android resource rules):

    inter_regular.ttf
    inter_medium.ttf
    inter_semibold.ttf
    inter_bold.ttf
    inter_black.ttf   (Details title)

Source: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter -> "Get font" -> download.
In the zip, open static/ and copy Inter_18pt-Regular.ttf, Inter_18pt-Medium.ttf,
Inter_18pt-SemiBold.ttf, Inter_18pt-Bold.ttf and Inter_18pt-Black.ttf, then rename them as above.
Inter uses the SIL Open Font License, so you can bundle it in the app.
